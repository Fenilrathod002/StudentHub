Our Mission

StudentHub helps colleges create a connected learning environment where students, teachers, and administrators can work together smoothly.

Who Uses StudentHub

Students

Access assignments, attendances, notices, and course resources from one place.

Teachers

Share notes, update class records, and communicate with students quickly.

Administrators

Manage college information and publish announcements to all users.

Key Features

  • Attendance tracking
  • Assignment and study resource sharing
  • Announcements and messages
  • Personal student profile management

Role Overview

Role Primary Access
Student Dashboard, attendance, assignments
Teacher Class updates, teaching materials, student communication
Admin Institutional reports, notices, user management

StudentHub supports simpler academic management.
